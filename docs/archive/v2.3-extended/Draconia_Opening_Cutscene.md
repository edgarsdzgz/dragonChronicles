# 🐉 Prologue: The Day the Sky Broke

> In the age before silence, Draconia was not a fortress. It was a cradle.  
> Within its sky-spires, young dragons learned flight, flame, and the ten thousand disciplines of war and wonder.  
> We were the Academy’s final class — fledglings forged to defend the world beyond the capital walls.

> On the morning of our graduation, the skies themselves screamed.  
> The stars dimmed, and the horizon burned — not with fire, but with unmaking.  
> What the elders called *the Rift* tore through the outer lands, unweaving forest, ocean, and mountain alike.  
> In a single hour, every outpost, every eyrie, every teacher… vanished.

> Only the Barrier of Draconia held — the great ward powered by the world’s last reservoir of Arcana.  
> The Council fell silent within their sealed chambers. The forges went cold.  
> And in that silence, a single truth settled over us: **We were all that remained.**

> “Graduates of the Final Flight,” came the voice of the High Chronicler, fractured through the crystal relays,  
> “Draconia endures by your wings alone. The Rift spreads. The Barrier wanes. You must gather the scattered Arcana and rekindle the city’s heart.”

> Fly, Graduate of the Final Flight. The age of masters is over. The age of defenders begins.
